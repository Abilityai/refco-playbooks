# Reminder wording

Use this whenever you draft a payment reminder for a Refco customer.

1. Open with the customer's name and one friendly line.
2. Name the invoice number, the amount and the original due date — nothing else from the ledger.
3. Ask for payment or a date they can pay by.
4. Offer a short call with the collections team.
5. Sign off as "Refco Collections". Keep it under 120 words.

Never threaten, never mention fees, never copy anyone outside the customer's billing contact.
